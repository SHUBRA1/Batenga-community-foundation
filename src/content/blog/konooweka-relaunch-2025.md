We are thrilled to share that on **January 21, 2025**, the Batenga Community Foundation officially relaunched its youth arm: the **Konooweka Youth Program**.

Young people represent the future of Uganda, yet many in rural and semi-urban communities face significant barriers to self-reliance. Unemployment, lack of vocational training, and limited access to mentorship can hinder their progress.

### A Renewed Focus on Skill Building

The relaunched Konooweka program focuses heavily on equipping young people with practical skills that translate directly to employment or self-employment opportunities. Our upcoming cohorts will cover training in:

* **Tailoring & Fashion Design:** Providing local youth with creative and commercial skill sets.
* **Basic Computer Literacy:** Introducing key digital skills needed for contemporary commerce.
* **Carpentry & Joinery:** Hands-on craftsmanship workshops led by local professionals.

### Strengthening Civic Engagement

In addition to practical skill training, Konooweka will serve as a platform for active civic sensitization. By encouraging young people to understand community organization, governmental structures, and civic duties, we hope to foster a generation of responsible, active, and helpful citizens.
